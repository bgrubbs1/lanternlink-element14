# Verified contest requirements

Official page:
https://community.element14.com/challenges-projects/project14/p/make-a-connection

Verified from the official Element14 Community page on 2026-08-09.

## Theme and entry

- Build an electronics project that sends a message or signal.
- Submit one project blog.
- Share the build step by step.
- Explain how the project relates to sending a message or signal.
- Demonstrate a complete and functional project with video and photographs.
- Demonstrate originality, innovation, and technical merit.

## Dates

- Deadline: 2026-09-13 at 23:59 UK time (18:59 EDT)
- Winners announced: 2026-09-18
- Prizes shipped: September 2026

## Prize structure

- one first-place winner chooses a Steam Deck OLED, 100 MHz oscilloscope, or
  Dino-Lite digital microscope;
- three second-place winners choose a 20 MHz oscilloscope, Nintendo Switch, or
  Wera torque-driver set;
- three runners-up choose a Dremel kit, Wera torque screwdriver, or Multicomp
  crimp-tool kit.

## Promotion gate

- [x] real cross-device signal test;

- [x] privacy/IP audit and automated tests;
- [x] genuine sanitized functional video;
