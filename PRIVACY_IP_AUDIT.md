# Privacy and IP boundary

Audit started: 2026-08-09

## Permitted public evidence

- original LanternLink source and documentation;
- generic node labels such as `WORKSHOP-1` and `HARBOR-1`;
- loopback examples;
- sanitized output from a deliberately generated contest test;
- an original architecture diagram;
- footage of the dedicated LanternLink interface only.

## Explicit exclusions

- supplied phone/email, home address, family details, or precise location;
- private LAN addresses, hostnames, MAC addresses, Wi-Fi names, credentials,
  tokens, secrets, or full network topology;
- employer/customer/work-project code, screenshots, data, or descriptions;
- workplace application captures, meeting material, or employer data;
- unrelated radio-observation data, device identifiers,
  house/vehicle/presence observations, or neighborhood captures;
- unrelated Operations Center, terminal, browser, or desktop content;
- fabricated hardware photos, delivery claims, security claims, test results,
  or message traffic.

## Recording rules

- record a purpose-built browser window with only the LanternLink page visible;
- use generic sender/message data and a neutral background;
- hide the address bar, desktop, notifications, bookmarks, and other tabs;
- do not film serial numbers, mail, screens, family members, or workplace
  material;
- describe the two owned computers generically unless the entrant explicitly
  approves model disclosure;
- keep the HMAC secret and private LAN address outside video and screenshots.

## Authorship

The code, diagram, blog, and video layout may be AI-assisted. The entrant
reviews the final public entry, confirms the project represents the work they
intend to publish, and remains responsible for all claims and account/legal
terms.
